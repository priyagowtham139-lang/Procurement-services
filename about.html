About Stackly

We build the infrastructure procurement teams deserve.

Founded by former public-sector buyers, Stackly exists to make sourcing transparent, fast and defensible — for governments and enterprises alike.

Our mission

Procurement should be a public trust, not a paper trail.

We started Stackly after years inside city procurement offices, watching good policy get lost in spreadsheets and email chains. We build software that makes the right way the easy way.
